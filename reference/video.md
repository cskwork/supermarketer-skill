# Video playbook

## Video brief

Record:

- objective and desired action,
- audience and awareness,
- placement and current spec,
- duration,
- core promise/proof,
- opening mechanism,
- narrative structure,
- CTA/end card,
- sound-on/sound-off behavior,
- brand/rights constraints,
- output variants.

## Required production files

- script,
- time-coded storyboard,
- shot list,
- voiceover,
- on-screen text,
- asset/prompt list,
- captions/subtitles,
- thumbnail/first-frame,
- edit decision notes,
- rights manifest.

## Rendering

When a renderer/editor exists:

- produce master and requested variants,
- inspect full playback and frame grabs,
- verify metadata and captions,
- record adapter and files.

When unavailable:

```text
Render status: PRODUCTION_PACK_ONLY
```

Deliver the complete production pack. Never produce a fake video file or imply a render occurred.

## Review

- relevance established early,
- visual story works without sound where required,
- text display time is readable,
- claims/demos are truthful,
- continuity and pacing,
- audio level and clarity,
- captions and safe zones,
- final CTA/end card,
- duration/dimensions/container/frame rate/file size.
