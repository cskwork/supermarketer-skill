# Positioning and message-house playbook

## Positioning sequence

1. Identify the priority segment.
2. Name the buying situation/JTBD.
3. List realistic alternatives, including doing nothing or manual work.
4. Establish the category frame.
5. Identify unique attributes.
6. Translate attributes into differentiated value.
7. Attach reasons to believe.
8. Record objections, limitations, and prohibited claims.
9. Write the positioning statement.
10. Build the message house.

## Positioning statement format

For `[priority audience]` who `[buying situation/problem]`, `[product]` is a `[category/frame]` that `[differentiated value]`. Unlike `[primary alternative]`, it `[unique attribute/reason to believe]`.

This is an internal decision tool, not mandatory public copy.

## Message house

- Core promise.
- Pillar 1 + proof.
- Pillar 2 + proof.
- Pillar 3 + proof.
- Objection handling.
- CTA.
- Unsupported/prohibited language.

## Gate

Positioning must be:

- true,
- relevant,
- differentiated,
- defensible,
- usable in channel copy,
- and no broader than the evidence.
