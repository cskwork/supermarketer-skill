# Marketing accessibility playbook

Accessibility is part of production quality.

## Static

- sufficient text/background contrast,
- body text readable at destination size,
- no information conveyed by color alone,
- meaningful alt text,
- avoid over-dense small text,
- preserve hierarchy under zoom/crop.

## Video

- accurate captions,
- readable on-screen text duration,
- safe zones,
- meaningful audio description or transcript when required,
- avoid flashing or harmful motion,
- avoid essential information only in audio,
- control background music under speech.

## Motion

- respect reduced-motion requirements in interactive assets,
- do not use motion as the sole cue,
- keep transitions purposeful.

## Language

- plain, audience-appropriate language,
- expansion/reflow for localization,
- avoid unexplained jargon.
