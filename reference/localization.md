# Localization and transcreation playbook

## Principle

Adapt the communication effect, not the word order.

## Required checks

- target market/audience,
- formality and tone,
- local category language,
- idioms/metaphors,
- proof and examples,
- date/time/number/currency/unit formats,
- CTA conventions,
- local channel behavior,
- imagery and symbolism,
- legal/policy disclosures,
- text expansion and layout,
- subtitle and voiceover timing.

## Claims

Re-evaluate every claim in the target market. Evidence, approvals, competitor references, and required qualifiers may differ.

## Output

- localized copy/assets,
- adaptation notes,
- changed claim IDs,
- local source evidence,
- back-translation/meaning check for high-risk text,
- re-rendered files,
- independent local review.

Literal translation without reflow is not a completed localized creative.
