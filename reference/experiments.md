# Creative and message experiment playbook

## Hypothesis

Use:

> For `[audience/placement]`, changing `[one primary variable]` from `[control]` to `[variant]` will improve `[primary KPI]` because `[mechanism/evidence]`.

## Design

Record:

- control,
- variants,
- one primary variable,
- audience and placement,
- traffic/exposure method,
- primary metric,
- guardrails,
- practical decision threshold or MDE when possible,
- sample/exposure assumptions,
- duration/seasonality,
- stop rule,
- analysis plan.

## Rules

- Do not change multiple strategic variables and call it a clean A/B test.
- Do not select the success metric after seeing results.
- Separate exploratory tests from confirmatory tests.
- Keep variants truthful and policy-compliant.
- A pre-launch model or reviewer score may prioritize variants, not validate performance.

## Decision

Predeclare scale/iterate/stop conditions and the next action for inconclusive results.
