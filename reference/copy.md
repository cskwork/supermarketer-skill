# Copy playbook

## Copy order

1. Audience and context.
2. Desired action.
3. Primary promise.
4. Proof.
5. Objection/qualifier.
6. CTA.
7. Channel constraints.

## Rules

- Specificity beats hype.
- Benefits must remain tied to product truth.
- Use the audience's language only when supported by research.
- Avoid vague filler: “revolutionary,” “seamless,” “next-level,” “unlock,” and similar language unless made concrete.
- Do not use fear, urgency, scarcity, or social proof falsely.
- Preserve material terms and qualifiers.
- Match awareness level and channel intent.
- Keep one primary CTA.
- Write variants with a named difference, not synonyms.

## Factual claims

Every factual, quantitative, comparative, testimonial, or promotional claim must have a `CLAIMS.yaml` ID.

## Common deliverables

- headline/subhead/body/CTA,
- paid ad variants,
- organic social copy,
- landing sections,
- email subject/preheader/body,
- push/in-app,
- script/voiceover/on-screen text,
- metadata/alt text.

## Review

Read aloud, scan at channel size, check limits, remove unsupported statements, and test whether the CTA describes an actual next step.
