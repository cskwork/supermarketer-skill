# Image production playbook

## Define the image job

- purpose in the campaign,
- subject and action,
- product truth to preserve,
- visual style,
- composition and negative space,
- aspect ratio and crop variants,
- exclusions,
- brand palette,
- rights and likeness constraints.

## Adapter chain

1. native image generation/editing,
2. approved external provider,
3. licensed/user-provided source,
4. vector/SVG creation,
5. prompt/art-direction pack only.

Record the tier and substitutions.

## Inspection

Inspect at full size and destination crop:

- product geometry/details,
- logos/packaging,
- text,
- interfaces,
- people/hands/faces,
- reflections/shadows,
- repeated/merged objects,
- background artifacts,
- prohibited content,
- visual truthfulness.

## Rules

- Never fabricate a customer, testimonial, product capability, data screen, or award.
- No unauthorized real-person likeness.
- No copyrighted character or third-party trademark without permission.
- Disclose AI-generated status in the manifest.
- A fallback prompt pack is not a generated image.
