# Brand, claims, rights, privacy, and compliance

## Brand

Read existing rules first:

- positioning and category,
- voice and tone,
- approved/prohibited language,
- logo and clearspace,
- color/type/image/motion system,
- audience and market,
- accessibility,
- co-branding.

When no brand exists, create a bounded campaign direction; do not silently define a permanent brand system.

## Claims

Every material external claim requires an evidence mapping and approval state. Preserve scope, qualifiers, and material terms.

Block:

- invented or outdated product facts,
- unsupported absolute/comparative claims,
- synthetic testimonials/social proof,
- misleading demos,
- hidden promotion conditions.

## Rights

Track license/permission for:

- images,
- footage,
- music,
- voice,
- fonts,
- logos,
- customer marks,
- likeness,
- locations/property.

## Privacy

Redact personal/confidential data before external generation. Do not use customer lists, private recordings, unreleased assets, or internal metrics without authorization.

## Regulated contexts

Research current jurisdiction/platform rules. The skill provides risk flags and evidence, not a substitute for authorized legal review.
