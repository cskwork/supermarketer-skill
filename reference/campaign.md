# Campaign playbook

## Campaign contract

A campaign connects a business objective to an audience journey and coordinated assets. It is not merely a list of channels.

## Required decisions

- Objective and funnel stage.
- Priority audience and insight.
- Offer and desired action.
- Big idea/creative concept.
- Message sequence.
- Channel roles.
- Asset matrix.
- Timing/sequence.
- KPI and guardrails.
- Experiment/learning plan.
- Non-goals.

## Channel role examples

Choose based on evidence, not a fixed funnel:

- introduce problem/category,
- demonstrate product,
- establish proof,
- handle objection,
- capture demand,
- activate user,
- retain/expand,
- retarget/reassure.

## Asset matrix

Every asset maps to:

- audience,
- buying situation,
- message,
- proof,
- CTA,
- channel/placement,
- format,
- owner,
- status,
- KPI.

## Concept exploration

When requested, create 2–4 genuinely different territories. Each includes:

- strategic angle,
- headline territory,
- visual/narrative system,
- proof mechanism,
- channel fit,
- risk.

Recommend one. Do not blend all territories into a generic compromise.
