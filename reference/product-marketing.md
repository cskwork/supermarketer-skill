# Product marketing playbook

## Minimum model

- Priority segment and role in purchase.
- Buying situation or JTBD.
- Problem, stakes, and existing alternative.
- Category frame.
- Unique product attributes.
- Differentiated value.
- Reasons to believe.
- Objections and limitations.
- Offer and desired action.
- Funnel stage and channel role.
- Primary KPI and guardrails.

## Output discipline

A small asset request needs only the strategy required to make the asset correct. A launch or repositioning request may require the full model.

## Product truth

Create or reference approved facts before copy production:

- capabilities,
- limitations,
- pricing/availability,
- proof/data,
- integrations/compatibility,
- customer evidence,
- certification/awards,
- geographic/legal constraints.

Unknown facts stay unknown.

## Message by awareness

Adapt the same truth to:

- problem-unaware,
- problem-aware,
- solution-aware,
- product-aware,
- most-aware/current customer.

Do not change truth or invent urgency; change context, explanation, proof, and CTA.
